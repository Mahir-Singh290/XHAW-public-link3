About Us

Our Story

Adventure Escape SA was founded in 2024 by Liam Daniels, who saw that families, tourists, schools and companies had no single place to explore and compare guided outdoor experiences. We bring zip lining, kayaking, rock climbing and more together across the Western Cape, so booking an adventure is simple.

Meet The Founder

Liam is the founder of Adventure Escape SA. His passion for outdoor adventure and eco-tourism inspired him to build a business that lets people of all ages and experience levels enjoy the Western Cape safely and responsibly.

Our Western Cape Roots

Every experience takes place in the Western Cape, across its mountains, rivers and coastline, led by guides who know the area well.

Safety First

Every activity is professionally guided, and each guest receives a safety briefing and the right equipment before they start.

Environmental Responsibility

We practise eco-tourism by keeping groups small, staying on marked trails and leaving nature as we found it.
